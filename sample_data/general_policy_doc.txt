=======================================================
HOSPITAL DATA GOVERNANCE & ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
=======================================================
Document Code    : POL-2026-HOSP-01
Classification   : Internal Hospital Administrative Policy
Effective Date   : 2026-01-01
Review Date      : 2026-12-31

1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This policy defines the access authorization boundaries for electronic medical
records (EMRs) across clinical departments, including Cardiology, Radiology,
and Central Administration.

2. ACCESS TIERS
- Department Isolation: Medical practitioners may only query and view records
  originating from their assigned clinical specialty.
- Central Oversight: The Hospital Security & Compliance Administrator has
  supervisory clearance to audit system activity, examine logs, and view records.
- Violation Logging: Any attempt to query records outside of an authorized
  department is classified as an Unauthorized Access Event and permanently
  recorded in the security audit database.

3. COMPLIANCE & PENALTIES
Failure to observe patient data isolation boundaries incurs immediate audit review
and potential credential revocation.
